# Pilot 0 Screening Results

These files contain **screening-stage results**, not confirmatory evidence.

The underlying agent and task are based on the mixed Active Inference model introduced by:

Paul, A., Isomura, T., & Razi, A. (2024).
*On Predictive Planning and Counterfactual Learning in Active Inference.*
Entropy, 26(6), 484. https://doi.org/10.3390/e26060484

The original DPEFE planner, Counterfactual Learning pathway, Beta arbitration mechanism,
and mutating-grid implementation are upstream contributions of Paul et al.

## Experimental question

The present extension asks whether the value of fresh predictive planning depends on
planning depth and context.

Counterfactual evaluation compared:

`N ∈ {5, 10, 25, 50}`

from matched pre-planning checkpoints.

These tables were generated during **Pilot 0**, whose purpose was to determine whether
the planning-depth manipulation produced enough policy and behavioural variation to
justify further meta-control analysis.

## Important limitations

Pilot 0 used:

- 2 screening base seeds,
- 30 checkpoints,
- 3 selection and 3 TEST rollouts per depth/checkpoint condition,
- a 15,000-action cap,
- substantial censoring in parts of the hard regime.

The dataset was explicitly designated as **screening** and was not intended to support
final confirmatory statistical claims.

The main conclusion supported by these tables is therefore limited:

> Planning depth materially affected policy and behaviour in the hard regime, but the
> experiment does not by itself establish a successful internal meta-control mechanism.

## Files

### `phase_policy_sensitivity.csv`

Phase-level summaries of pairwise differences between fresh DPEFE policies generated
at different planning depths.

Metrics include:

- start-state total variation distance,
- start-state Jensen-Shannon divergence,
- mean and maximum state-wise total variation,
- fraction of modeled states with different argmax actions.

This table supports the conclusion that planning-depth interventions were not
policy-invariant in the hard regime.

### `phase_censoring_summary.csv`

Phase × planning-depth summaries of:

- action-cap rate,
- success rate,
- capped episode length,
- number of rollouts and checkpoints.

This table documents the major behavioural limitation of Pilot 0: substantial
right-censoring in parts of the hard regime.

### `phase_primary_resolution.csv`

For each phase, reports the fraction of checkpoints at which the primary behavioural
outcome varied sufficiently across planning depths to resolve a depth effect.

This is a screening diagnostic rather than a confirmatory statistical result.

### `H1_vs_H3_summary.csv`

Comparison between early-hard (`H1`) and late-hard (`H3`) conditions in the same hard
maze.

This comparison was designed to reduce simple maze-geometry confounding when asking
whether planning-depth sensitivity changes with learning context.

## Interpretation boundary

These files support:

- planning depth can change the policy generated by the upstream DPEFE planner,
- behavioural consequences of depth vary across learning regimes,
- hard-regime censoring is substantial.

They do **not** establish:

- that the agent possesses metacognition,
- that the agent can infer the optimal planning depth from its internal state,
- that an adaptive planning controller generalizes out of sample,
- or that Pilot 0 constitutes confirmatory evidence.

See the repository-level documentation for the distinction between the upstream model
and the adaptive-planning extension.
